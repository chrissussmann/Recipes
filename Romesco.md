### Romesco

This quick romesco takes 5 minutes to make, and is the perfect sauce for chicken, steak, pork, potatoes, and vegetables. It's healthy and full of flavor!

**Ingredients**
- 16 oz jar roasted red peppers 12oz dry weight
- 1/2 cup toasted slivered almonds (or nut of choice)
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 3/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper

**Instructions**
1. Place all ingredients into a food processor or blender, and blend until smooth.
2. A food processor will make for a chunkier sauce, whereas the blender will make for a smoother sauce.
3. Store leftovers in the refrigerator for 1 week.

**Notes**
 Yields 2 cups
